**1. Screen recording**

The app has no account registration, no login, no user-generated content shared with other users, no paid content, and no in-app purchases.

**2. Purpose and target audience**
Mhysai is a personal finance and motivation tool for salaried employees in Türkiye. Users enter their net or gross monthly salary and their working schedule; the app converts the salary into real-time earnings and shows, second by second, how much they have earned today, this month and this year. It also shows how many working days common expenses (rent, bills) or things they want to buy correspond to, and lets users log breaks (tea, meetings, etc.) to see what that time is worth.
The value it provides is making the value of one's working time tangible: it helps people understand their hourly/daily earnings, how Turkish income tax brackets reduce net pay during the year, and what purchases cost in terms of working time. Target audience: adult office and salaried workers in Türkiye. The app's interface is in Turkish.

**3. Setup and accessing the main features**
No login, credentials or sample files are required.
- Launch the app → tap "Hadi başlayalım" → enter any monthly salary (e.g. 60000) → keep the default schedule (Mon–Fri, 09:00–18:00) → tap "Sayacı başlat".
- "Sayaç" tab: live counter. It only increases during the configured working hours. If you review outside those hours (or on a weekend/public holiday in Türkiye), go to "Ayarlar" (Settings), set start 00:00 and end 23:59, select all seven days, and the counter will run immediately.
- Amounts are hidden (****) by default for privacy at the workplace; tap the eye icon at the top right to reveal them.
- "Kaytarma" tab: tap + / − to add or remove 5 minutes for a break type; the share icon creates a summary image.
- "Kaç Mesai?" tab: tap a suggestion chip (e.g. "Kira") or + to add an expense or wish item and see its equivalent in working days and salaries.
- "Ayarlar" tab: switch "Net / Brüt" to "Brüt" and tap the summary row to see the 12-month gross-to-net payroll breakdown.

**4. External services**
None. The app does not use any external services, SDKs, servers, data providers, authentication, payment processors, analytics, advertising or AI services. All data is stored locally on the device (UserDefaults) and the app works fully offline. The only links in the app open our static privacy policy and support pages.

**5. Regional differences**
The app functions identically in all regions. Its content is designed for Türkiye: the interface is Turkish, amounts are in Turkish lira, and the built-in public holiday calendar and gross-to-net payroll calculation (social security premiums, income tax brackets, stamp tax, minimum wage exemption) follow Turkish rules. There is no region-based feature gating.

**6. Regulated industry / third-party material**
The app does not operate in a regulated industry and does not provide financial, tax, payroll or investment services. Salary and tax figures are clearly labeled in the app as informational estimates only. The app does not contain protected third-party material.
